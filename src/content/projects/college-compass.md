## The brief

Moodle holds everything a student needs and makes all of it hard to find. My classmates and I were losing time hunting for lecture material and missing coursework dates.

## The work

College Compass gathers course information, lectures and coursework due dates into a single, calm layout that answers "what do I need this week?" at a glance. The visual design borrows from Frutiger Aero: glossy, airy and optimistic, deliberately unlike a university portal.

It's a static site on GitHub Pages, so it's fast, free to host and easy for anyone in the class to open on their phone.
