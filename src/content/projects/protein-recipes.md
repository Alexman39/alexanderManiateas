## The brief

Clients always ask the same question: what should I actually eat? The coach needed a place to send them, with simple recipes and the numbers that matter, in Greek.

## The work

The site presents each recipe as a card with its calories, protein, prep time and tags, and lets you filter by meal and sort by protein, calories or speed. It's a static site in the same visual family as the coach's main website, and it's built to grow from free recipes into premium recipes and meal-prep guides.
