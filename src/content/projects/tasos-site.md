## The brief

A coach's website has to do two jobs: show who he is, and sell. It needed the presence of a bodybuilding brand, and a clear path to the programs and to coaching.

## The work

I designed and built the full site: fifteen pages, including the home page, the program catalogue and a page for each of the six programs, coaching, recipes, an about page and the legal pages. The style is bold black-and-white photography with a single bright accent, so the programs stand out.

It's the public face of the work I do with Tasos, alongside [FlexPro Coaching](../projects/flexpro), the [protein recipes](../projects/protein-recipes) site and the [nutrition plan builder](../projects/nutrition-plan-builder).
