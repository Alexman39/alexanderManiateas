## The brief

Online coaching lives or dies on the relationship between coach and client. FlexPro needed a platform that carries that relationship: where clients land, sign up, follow their programme and stay in touch, without the software getting in the way.

## The work

I co-develop the platform end to end with the coach: the structure of the product, the user experience, and the client journey from first visit to ongoing coaching. Behind it, I'm building the systems that let the coaching business scale beyond what one person can manage by hand.

It's built in Flutter on Firebase and is currently in alpha, so this folio is a case study rather than a source repository.
