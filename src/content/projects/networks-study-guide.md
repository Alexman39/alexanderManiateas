## The brief

CN5002 packs a lot into one exam: subnetting, the OSI model, router configuration, RIP, VLANs and network simulation. I wanted to turn the whole module into a plan you can actually follow in the days before the exam.

## The work

The guide is split into five days. Day 1 covers IPv4 and subnetting, day 2 the OSI model and router configuration, day 3 RIP v1, day 4 VLANs and network services, and day 5 simulation and a final review. Each day has three tabs: the lecture, exercises that check your answers, and exam-style practice.

It's a single static page with a light and a dark theme, so it opens instantly on a phone at the library. You can try it right here.
